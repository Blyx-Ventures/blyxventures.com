## Source and method

- **Equipment reference:** User-supplied open network-rack photograph, used for the rack construction, equipment hierarchy, device density, and cable organization. The casters, top wireless unit, surrounding room, furniture, branding, labels, and photographic treatment were not reproduced.
- **Visual-style reference:** RFM Group office-layout wireframe supplied by the user, used only for its partial-solid technical-render language, fine construction lines, pale field, and controlled depth. The office subject, room plan, furniture, composition, and specific content were not reproduced.
- **Generation method:** OpenAI built-in image generation, iterative edit workflow
- **Production direction:** One audience-neutral stationary network rack on the right, redrawn from the equipment reference in a front-and-right three-quarter view. Keep the complete equipment face and front frame solid, detailed, and dominant. Retain one fitted right-side panel as a continuous pale semi-transparent plane with precise graphite perimeter lines and restrained construction seams. The panel masks all internal chassis bodies, shelves, rails, and rack depth. Use thin organized cabling and one small green cable accent.

## Production constraints

- The asset remains audience-neutral and subordinate to the hero promise.
- The image contains no embedded text, logo, fake interface, people, or identifiable property.
- The render is marketing context, not authentic Blyx project evidence.
- The website keeps essential copy and actions outside the image.
- Wide hero layouts enlarge the rack uniformly without changing its perspective, keep both left-side corners inset from the vertical section boundaries, and intentionally clip the right-side continuation. Compact stacked layouts preserve the complete rack and readable cable organization.
