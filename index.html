Our services

The foundation of a smarter space.

01

Networking

Dead zones, dropped connections, and unreliable Wi-Fi can make a home or workplace harder to use.

We plan and install wired and wireless networks around the space, including better coverage, dependable connections where they matter, and separate access for guests, cameras, or smart devices when useful. We also take on focused upgrades to improve an existing network.

Before we leave, we test the network and provide your system access and the information you need to use and manage it.

02

Security cameras

Security cameras should help you understand what happened, not leave you searching through unclear or missing footage.

For new systems, we plan what needs to be covered, install the wiring and cameras, set up recording, choose how long footage is kept, and configure phone access, user permissions, and supported activity alerts.

Before we leave, we test the system with you and show you how to view live video, review recordings, and manage access.

03

Access control and automated entry

Entering a home or workplace should be secure and work for the people who use it.

We plan and install compatible access control and automatic door-opening systems, with options such as interior buttons, key fobs, codes, RFID cards, fingerprint entry where supported, and remote operation.

Before we leave, we test the complete entry sequence, transfer administrative access, and show you how to use it.

Home network + security cameras · Private residence

Better Wi-Fi and a clearer view of the property.

Need
The customer wanted security cameras around the property, but the home’s Wi-Fi did not provide reliable coverage everywhere it was needed.
Scope
Blyx ran new wiring, improved Wi-Fi coverage, installed the cameras, and organized the equipment in one central location.
Result
The home gained more reliable connections, dependable camera recording, and a convenient way to view the cameras remotely.

Automated entry · Private residence

Independent access, built into the home.

Need
A wheelchair user needed a way to enter and leave through two residential doors without assistance—and to let visitors in without traveling to the door.
Scope
Blyx installed powered swing-door operators at two entrances and integrated flexible authorized-entry and remote-operation controls around the resident’s daily use.
Result
The resident can enter and exit independently and open the door for visitors remotely.
