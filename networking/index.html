When a better network matters

Connections should be dependable throughout the space.

Wi-Fi does not reach everywhere

Dead zones, weak signals, and dropped connections make everyday devices harder to use.

Some devices need a wire

Workstations, televisions, cameras, and other fixed equipment often benefit from a dependable Ethernet connection.

The space is changing

A renovation, addition, or new build is the right time to plan cabling and equipment locations before walls are closed.

The network needs a defined upgrade

Existing equipment and wiring can be reorganized or replaced as part of a planned improvement with clear goals.

Planning the system

What we evaluate.

We look at the layout and construction of the property, where coverage is needed, which devices should be wired, practical cable routes, and whether existing equipment is suitable for the finished system.

  • Wi-Fi coverage and access-point placement
  • Ethernet runs for work areas, cameras, televisions, and other fixed devices
  • Router, switch, and firewall installation and configuration
  • Guest access and useful separation for cameras or smart devices
  • Clean organization in a rack or structured-media enclosure

Common questions

Planning a network project.

Does Blyx provide internet service?

No. Your internet provider brings service to the property; Blyx plans and installs the network that distributes that connection throughout the space.

Can Ethernet be installed in a finished space?

Often, yes. We evaluate accessible routes and explain what is practical before the scope is approved.

Should I use wired connections, Wi-Fi, or both?

Most projects benefit from both. Fixed devices can use wired connections while phones, tablets, and mobile devices use planned Wi-Fi coverage.

Can existing equipment be retained?

Compatible, dependable equipment may be incorporated when it supports the agreed design. We confirm that during discovery.

Can different devices use separate network access?

When the selected equipment supports it, guest access, cameras, and smart devices can be separated to make access easier to manage.
