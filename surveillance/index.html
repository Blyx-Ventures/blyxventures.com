Before you get started

You may be wondering…

Can I check cameras from my phone?

Supported systems let you view live video and recordings remotely. We set up access and show you how to use it.

How long are recordings kept?

That depends on the number of cameras, recording settings, image quality, and storage. We plan retention around how far back you want to look.

Will the cameras record if the internet goes down?

A properly designed system can continue recording locally while power and the local network remain available. Remote viewing and online alerts need an internet connection.

Can I see what happens at night?

We evaluate available light and camera placement before choosing equipment. The detail you can see depends on lighting, distance, and movement; some areas may need additional lighting.

Before you get started

You may be wondering…

Can I check cameras from my phone?

Yes. Supported systems let authorized users view live video and recordings remotely. We configure access and show you how to use it.

How long is footage kept?

Retention depends on camera count, recording settings, image quality, and storage capacity. We plan these around how far back you need to look.

What happens if the internet goes down?

A properly designed system can keep recording locally while power and the local network remain available. Remote viewing and online alerts need a working internet connection.

Can cameras capture useful footage at night?

Yes, with the right camera and placement. We evaluate available light and the areas you need to see, then choose cameras suited to those conditions. Image detail depends on lighting, distance, and movement; some areas may need additional lighting.
