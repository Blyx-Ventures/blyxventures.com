Common questions

What to expect from a new system.

Can I view the cameras from my phone?

Remote viewing can be configured when the selected system, network, and internet connection support it. We verify access during handoff.

How long is footage kept?

Retention depends on camera count, recording mode, resolution, storage, and the goals agreed during planning.

Do cameras work when the internet is down?

Local recording may continue when the equipment has power and the local network is operating, but remote viewing and alerts typically depend on internet service.

Can Blyx add to my existing camera system?

We focus on complete new systems and do not take over, repair, troubleshoot, or expand systems installed by others.
